Electrical testing and maintenance

Test it before it fails. Maintain it so it keeps running.

On-site testing and maintenance for industrial and commercial facilities, with a written record of every result.

What we test

Insulation resistance
Checks cables, motors and transformers for insulation breakdown.
Earthing
Measures earth resistance to confirm a safe fault path.
Transformers
Ratio, winding resistance and oil condition checks.
Circuit breakers
Contact resistance, trip timing and operation.
Protection relays
Secondary injection to confirm settings and trip behaviour.
Thermal inspection
Infrared scanning to find hot connections early.

How we maintain

Preventive maintenance
Scheduled inspection, cleaning, tightening and testing.
Breakdown repair
Fault finding and repair when a system stops.
Shutdown support
Planned work inside your shutdown window.
Annual contracts
A fixed visit schedule so nothing is missed.

Every visit ends with a report: test results for each asset, what passed and what did not, and recommendations in order of priority.
